My Role & Contribution

Conceived and shipped ScanSeek AI as sole developer and product owner. Built a native iOS app using SwiftUI and Apple's Vision framework for on-device OCR that extracts text from screenshots, auto-categorizes them (Shopping, Travel, Finance, Food, Code, etc.), and provides AI-powered explanations. Implemented multi-language OCR support, smart search ranking with exact and partial match scoring, and duplicate screenshot detection. All processing runs on-device for complete user privacy — no accounts or cloud sync required.
